Scope and limits

PSI combustion investigations and cabin materials, compared against NASA space fire experiments. Evidence review, not cabin certification.

Prevention · Detection · Suppression: each investigation and label is read for what it tells a crew about preventing, detecting or putting out a cabin fire.

Labels use verified evidence rows only. Unverified rows stay inspectable in the evidence panel but never produce a label; with 0 verified rows, results report that no verified evidence is loaded.

Partial gravity (Moon 0.16 g, Mars 0.38 g): no partial-gravity data in PSI yet. REDLINE shows those cases only as an estimate beside 0 g and 1 g results, never as a lunar measurement.
