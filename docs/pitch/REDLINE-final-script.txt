NASA burned acrylic aboard the space station. Now imagine you are choosing an acrylic panel for a lunar rover. Can you use that experiment to understand your panel?

After Apollo 1, NASA changed both cabin materials and the launch-pad atmosphere. The lesson is simple: the material matters, but so do the conditions.
Finding the word “acrylic” in a NASA experiment is a starting point. It is not a cabin safety verdict.

REDLINE takes you from a cabin material to an experiment you can cite, shows what that experiment cannot establish, and helps you identify the test you still need to run.
