## Caveats

- Unknown values are not zero. Initial/final calibrated O2 readings are not oxygen extinction limits.
- Material IDs group forms and grades. Check full material names, geometry, airflow, extraction notes and endpoint definitions in the linked CSV and cited sources; these are omitted from the compact table.
- Simulated lunar acceleration is not an on-Moon observation. Any lunar behavior estimate must be clearly labelled and bounded by 0 g and 1 g results; do not treat a limit or ignition result as a spread test.
- U25-01-L, U25-02-L, U25-03-L, U25-04-L, U25-06-L: simulated lunar acceleration in centrifuge; buoyant velocity unknown
- U25-01-L, U25-02-L, U25-03-L, U25-04-L, U25-06-L: Uncertainty: editorial plot-read allowance +/-0.5 pp O2; experimental uncertainty not stated; method describes 1 pp oxygen separation

- OF12-NOMEX-L-MOCSTAR, OF12-NOMEX-L-ULOISTAR, OF12-NOMEX-1G-MOC, OF12-NOMEX-1G-ULOI, OF12-NOMEX-0G-MOCSTAR, OF12-NOMEX-0G-ULOISTAR: Uncertainty: not stated for this table entry; MOC and ULOI are distinct endpoints, not error bars
